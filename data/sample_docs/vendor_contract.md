# Master Vendor Services Agreement: DataStream Analytics & Apex Logistics

## Clause 1: Scope of Services & Key Deliverables
DataStream Analytics agrees to deliver 24/7 predictive fleet analytics, vehicle telemetry ingestion, and route optimization APIs to Apex Logistics under the terms of this Agreement. DataStream shall maintain API availability uptime of not less than 99.95% measured over any rolling thirty (30) calendar day window, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance.

## Clause 2: Service Level Agreements (SLA) & Liquidated Credits
In the event that the monthly uptime falls below the 99.95% commitment, Apex Logistics shall be entitled to service credits calculated as follows:
- 99.00% to 99.94% uptime: 10% credit against the applicable monthly billing amount.
- 95.00% to 98.99% uptime: 25% credit against the applicable monthly billing amount.
- Below 95.00% uptime: 50% credit against the applicable monthly billing amount, plus the unilateral right for Apex Logistics to terminate this Agreement without penalty within 30 days of the SLA breach.

## Clause 3: Payment Terms, Billing Schedule & Penalties
Apex Logistics shall pay a fixed platform licensing fee of $12,500 USD per calendar month, billed quarterly in advance. Invoices issued by DataStream are payable Net 30 days from the invoice issuance date. Undisputed past-due balances accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month or the highest statutory rate permitted under Delaware law, whichever is lower.

## Clause 4: Data Security, Confidentiality & Breach Notification
DataStream Analytics warrants that all telemetry data transmitted by Apex Logistics is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 standards. In the event of an unauthorized access event or confirmed security breach affecting Apex Logistics data, DataStream must formally notify the Apex Chief Information Security Officer in writing within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.
