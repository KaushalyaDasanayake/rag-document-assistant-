# Acme Technologies - Global Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

## Section 1: Working Hours & Remote Work Policy
Acme Technologies operates on a flexible 40-hour workweek. Standard core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone, during which team members must be reachable via company Slack. Employees are entitled to up to three (3) days of remote work per week with manager approval. Permanent fully-remote arrangements require written sign-off from both the department Vice President and the Chief People Officer.

## Section 2: Annual Leave & Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time regular employees accrue 20 days of paid annual vacation leave per calendar year, calculated at 1.67 days per full month worked. Unused vacation days may be rolled over into the next calendar year up to a strict maximum of five (5) days; any rollover days beyond 5 will expire without payout on March 31st. In addition to annual leave, Acme grants twelve (12) company-wide public holidays and ten (10) paid sick days annually. Sick leave exceeding three consecutive days requires a certified medical note from a licensed healthcare provider.

## Section 3: Professional Development & Equipment Stipend
Every full-time team member receives an annual learning stipend of $1,500 USD for professional development courses, technical certifications, and conference tickets. Reimbursement requests must be submitted through Expensify with receipts attached within 30 days of purchase. Additionally, newly hired remote employees receive a one-time home office equipment grant of $750 USD to purchase ergonomic monitors, chairs, and peripherals. Company-issued laptops remain the sole property of Acme Technologies and must be returned within 14 calendar days upon employment termination.

## Section 4: Termination & Notice Periods
Employment at Acme Technologies is at-will where permitted by law. For voluntary resignations, individual contributors are required to provide a minimum of two (2) weeks' formal written notice. Managers, Directors, and Executive-level staff must provide four (4) weeks' written notice. During the notice period, all company confidential information, source code repositories, and production credentials remain strictly protected under the Acme Confidentiality and Invention Assignment Agreement.
